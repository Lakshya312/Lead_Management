How to upload

1
Open Excel and create 3 columns
In row 1 (the header), type exactly: ProductName Category IsActive — each in its own column.
2
Fill in your products from row 2 onwards
Write the product name, its category (must already exist in the system), and Yes / No (or 1 / 0) for IsActive. You can leave IsActive blank — it defaults to Active.
3
Save as .xlsx or .xls, then upload below
Drag your file into the upload box, or click it to browse. Then hit Upload & Import. You'll see a result for every row telling you what was imported or what went wrong.
Tip: Category names must match exactly what's already in your system. Go to Products first to check which categories exist.
